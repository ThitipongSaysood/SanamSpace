# 2026-08-08 15:00 — POS: selling drinks at the counter

_Agent: Claude (Opus 5)_

## Goal
"เพิ่มระบบ pos ขายของด้วยได้มั้ย เช่น น้ำ" — a till for the front desk.

Two decisions taken with the venue before building: **track stock with low-stock warnings**, and accept
**cash + PromptPay QR** only (no wallet charge, no adding to a booking bill).

## Shape

Three tables, not two, because a sale is a financial record and a product is not:

- `products` — the current catalogue. Names and prices change.
- `product_sales` — one receipt.
- `product_sale_items` — **snapshots `name` and `unit_price`** at the moment of sale. Joining back to
  `products` for the price would rewrite last month's takings the first time someone edits a price. Same
  principle the invoice work already follows.

`product_id` on a line is nullable so a deleted product cannot take its own sales history with it; the
snapshot is what keeps the line readable.

## The bug this was built to avoid

`PosService::sell` reads and writes stock **inside one transaction with `lockForUpdate()`**, and locks rows
in id order so two tills buying the same two items in opposite orders cannot deadlock. This project has
already shipped a double-booking race by doing read-then-write without a lock; two staff selling the last
bottle at once is that same bug with a different table, and it ends with negative stock.

Duplicate cart lines are **merged before the stock check** — two taps on the same bottle is one line of
two, and checking them separately would let each half pass a check the pair fails. Tested.

## Decisions worth keeping

- **Low stock warns, never blocks.** "Nearly out" is information; refusing a customer holding cash is not
  the till's call. Only zero stops a sale.
- **A void is a new fact, not an edit.** The receipt keeps its number, lines and seller and gains a reason.
  Deleting it would leave a hole in the day's takings nobody could explain. Voiding twice is refused, or
  the stock would come back twice.
- **Stock is not editable from the product form.** It moves through เติมของ (`delta` for a delivery, `set`
  for a stock-take), so editing a price cannot silently rewrite the count on the shelf.
- **Cashier and reception can sell but not void** — putting a sale back changes the day's takings, which is
  a supervisor's call. `pos.sell` / `pos.void` / `product.manage`.
- **PromptPay 422s when the venue has not configured an id**, rather than drawing a QR that pays nobody.

## Screens
- `/owner/pos` — the till. Tap to add, sticky cart, the two payment buttons are the largest things on the
  page. Transfer opens a real scannable QR for that sale's total, drawn from the venue's own PromptPay id.
- `/owner/products` — catalogue + stock, with a "ต้องเติมของ N รายการ" banner, which is the thing an owner
  opens this page to find out.
