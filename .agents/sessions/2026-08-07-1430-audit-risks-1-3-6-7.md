## Gotcha worth keeping
Route-level `throttle:5,1` on the login route **broke the multi-actor test flow**: ThrottleRequests
resolves `$request->user()` to key the limiter, which re-populates the sanctum guard with the leftover
`withToken()` bearer identity from a previous request — defeating the `forgetGuards()` the tests rely on,
so the owner request authenticated as the customer (403 "requires a staff account"). Doing the limit inside
the controller avoids touching the guard. In production (process-per-request) the middleware would have been
fine, but the in-controller version is also a better fit (per-account, failures-only).
